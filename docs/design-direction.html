PLATE 00
The concept

The Instrument.

The reference points are Apple × Foster + Partners × Arup × Leica — but the organising idea is drawn from the last of them. This practice does not design buildings; it examines surfaces at close range and finds, to a tolerance, what is wrong.

So the design is a precision measuring instrument: near-monochrome, gridded, annotated, tolerant to fractions of a millimetre — with a single index-red used the way it is used on a calibrated tool. It marks. It points. It never sells. Restraint is not a style choice here; for a practice whose highest-value work is independent expert testimony, restraint is the credibility.

PLATE 01
Colour — paper, ink & one signal

Ninety-five per cent of every screen is warm-neutral paper and near-black ink. Pure white reads as unstyled default; a warmed neutral reads as chosen. The instrument red is held to 1–2% of any view — annotation marks, active states, focus. The moment it becomes a “brand colour” splashed on buttons, the work becomes a template.

Image direction — evidence, not architecture

The category default — glossy photographs of finished buildings — is a trap: generic, and not what this practice does. The ownable, un-fakeable visual asset is the forensic detail itself, shot like scientific documentation and annotated with measurement.

PLATE 05
Spacing & grid — the graticule underneath

A 12‑column grid, wide outer margins, a hard ~1200px measure — the whitespace lives in the margins and in the vertical rhythm between sections. PLATE 06
Motion — an instrument settling

If you notice it,
it’s wrong.

The only routine motion is the restrained fade-and-rise you’ve been reading through — opacity and a 14px lift, mechanically eased, 400–600ms, no spring overshoot. Overshoot is the opposite of “measured.” One signature gesture per page; everything else holds still. prefers-reduced-motion is honoured completely.

PLATE 07
Decisions to lock before Phase 1

Five answers close every ambiguity.

  1. Name — a principal’s surname to build the mark around, or a coined abstract mark. Recommendation: a surname; expert credibility is personal.
  2. Signal accent — instrument red (recommended) or blueprint blue.
  3. Photography — accept forensic-over-architectural, and budget a small commissioned defect + portrait shoot.
  4. A serif for long-form report / Insights contexts — in or out.
  5. Content — confirm MDX-in-repo for launch, CMS deferred to real demand.
