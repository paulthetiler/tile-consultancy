PLATE 00
The metaphor — from the tool to the record

The Bureau
of Record.

Building→ Instrument→ Examination→ Record

The move is from claimed authority to demonstrated evidence. Authority you have to assert is authority you don’t yet have — so you stop claiming the last word and become the party that establishes the facts the last word is drawn from. You never call yourself the authority; you become the file the authority is drawn from.

“The Instrument” is demoted, not discarded. It was the wrong organising idea (a single tool produces a reading, not a finding, and can’t scale to a practice or an archive) but the right sensory one. The metrology aesthetic — graticule, the single red index line, monospace data, calliper photography — stays intact as the fingerprint of the method. The organising idea moves up to the institution that wields it: a standing evidentiary body, structured like barristers’ chambers — the mark scales, every inspector is named and personally accountable.

Rejected: “The Laboratory” — it over-claims accreditation (UKAS / ISO 17025) the practice doesn’t hold, fatal in the courtroom that matters most.

PLATE 01
Colour — paper, ink & one measured signal

The red is re-roled to a strictly metrological job: it marks only the measured value that constitutes a finding, and the out-of-tolerance condition. It leaves buttons, links and hovers — those revert to ink and hairline, where they were always meant to be. When you see red, something has been measured and something has failed. Conformance is never carried by colour alone — the word states it (no traffic-light green).

PLATE 04
The specimen — the atomic unit of evidence

One measured piece of evidence, rendered as a self-contained annotated object — authored once, reused everywhere: inside reports, in the deep-zoom evidence viewer, as illustration in the reference, on the homepage, and in the printed PDF. It is simultaneously proof, teaching object and SEO object — and the unit that makes both confidentiality-safe showcasing and multi-inspector scale possible.

Photography is shot like forensic documentation: focus-stacked macro, a low raking light to throw deviation into relief, an in-frame scale, the instrument’s readout legible, and the calibration wedge at the foot. The annotation layer is the design.

PLATE 05
The report is the product — sold like an instrument

Reports stop being downloadable afterthoughts and become the site’s central exhibit — a browsable, zoomable, annotated artefact whose craft is visible. You inspect the instrument before you commit. Document-control discipline is the first credibility signal: this is a controlled instrument, not a letter.

PLATE 06
The Reference — a controlled work, not a blog

Planned from day one as the industry’s definitive resource. The unit is the canonical, versioned, citable entry — never the chronological post. Commercial-tiling failure modes are a finite, enumerable field: own the complete enumeration at reference-work discipline and it reads as authoritative even while coverage is partial. Every entry is a controlled document — version, named reviewer, last-reviewed date, standards-watch link — and every real inspection feeds the corpus, so headcount growth is corpus growth.

FLAGSHIP SPINE
The Defect Atlas
One canonical monograph per failure mode — lippage, debonding, tenting, efflorescence, movement-joint failure. The most cited, most linkable asset.
QUANTITATIVE CANON
Tolerances & Datums
The authoritative tables — permissible lippage, surface regularity, deflection, moisture thresholds. Published first; marked up as citable Dataset.
INTERPRETIVE
Standards & Codes
Clause-level commentary on BS 5385, EN 12004/13888 — versioned so revisions are tracked, not silently overwritten.
METHOD
Test & Measurement
Repeatable procedures tied to the instrument pillar — pull-off, tap survey, moisture, flatness, slip (PTV).
EVIDENCE
Casework — redacted
Anonymised real investigations, cross-linked to every atlas, standard and tolerance they touch. The proprietary layer no rival can fabricate.
LANGUAGE
Nomenclature
A controlled vocabulary, defined once and auto-linked everywhere — DefinedTerm structured data across the corpus.

Five compounding moats: citation network · proprietary data · maintenance · completeness · links. The instruction flywheel spins at near-zero marginal cost.

PLATE 08
Motion — measurement gestures only

The site takes
a reading.

The only motions allowed are measurement gestures — the site behaves like an instrument taking a reading, never like a UI performing. A dimension line draws to a feature and the value resolves in the gap; a number settles to rest like a needle (never a hype odometer roll); a macro plate racks into focus. The signature moment, spent once or twice site-wide: a low raking light sweeps a hero surface, raising a defect’s relief, then settles to even light.

dimension draw value settle rack into focus raking sweep spring none

Hard prohibition: never animate a redaction lifting or a specimen un‑blurring — anonymisation must never look reversible. Reduced motion is the fully‑measured, fully‑sharp plate.

PLATE 09
Decisions to lock before Phase 1

Six answers close every ambiguity.

  1. Naming path — a coined, clearable institutional mark (recommended) over a seductive but unownable dictionary word like “Datum”. Brief a trademark clearance sprint first.
  2. Organising metaphor — confirm the Bureau of Record (Instrument demoted to method fingerprint).
  3. Reports to top-level nav, with the specimen viewer and synthetic-exemplar showcase.
  4. The Reference — day-one reference-work discipline; tolerance tables first.
  5. A one-day forensic photography commission (~8–12 plates + portrait) so launch runs on real evidence.
  6. Serif confirmed in — reports and the reference are hero products.
